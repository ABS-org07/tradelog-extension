What it does

Everything a trade journal should do.
Nothing you have to do yourself.

⚡

Zero-click capture

Place a trade. It appears in your journal instantly. No copy-pasting, no screenshots, no forms to fill.

📊

Auto P&L calculation

Your SELL is matched to your BUY automatically. P&L shown the moment you close a position.

📈

7-day performance chart

Green bars for profit days. Red bars for loss days. See your week at a glance without opening Excel.

Get started

Install in 2 minutes.

1

Visit the Chrome Web Store

Click the button below to open TradeLog on the Chrome Web Store. It's completely free.

2

Click "Add to Chrome"

You'll be taken to the Chrome Web Store. Click Add to Chrome → Add extension.

3

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome → find TradeLog → click the pin icon to keep it visible.

4

Start trading

Open Zerodha, Groww, Upstox or Angel One and place a trade. It appears in TradeLog automatically.
