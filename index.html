FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is TradeLog? +

TradeLog is a free Chrome extension that automatically logs every trade you place on Indian trading platforms like Zerodha, Groww, Upstox and Angel One. It captures the stock name, quantity, price, buy/sell direction and calculates P&L automatically — no manual entry needed.

Is TradeLog free? +

Yes, TradeLog is completely free. No signup required, no credit card, no subscription. Download it from the Chrome Web Store and start using it immediately.

Does TradeLog work with Zerodha Kite? +

Yes. TradeLog works seamlessly with Zerodha Kite. When you place a buy or sell order on kite.zerodha.com, TradeLog captures the trade automatically and logs it in your journal. No setup needed beyond installing the extension.

Where is my trade data stored? Is it safe? +

Your trade data is stored 100% locally in your Chrome browser using chrome.storage.local. It never leaves your device. No server, no cloud, no third party has access to your trades. Even the developer cannot see your data.

Can I export my trades to Excel? +

Yes. TradeLog has a one-click CSV export. All your trades — date, time, stock symbol, quantity, buy price, sell price, P&L and your emotion tags — are exported to a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

Does TradeLog work for F&O (options and futures) trading? +

Yes. TradeLog captures all trade types — equities, F&O (options and futures), intraday (MIS) and delivery (CNC). Whether you trade Nifty options, Bank Nifty futures or stock F&O, TradeLog logs every order automatically.

What is the Debrief tab? +

The Debrief tab is TradeLog's daily review feature. At the end of the day it shows you why you lost — whether it was overtrading, a single big loss, or a low win rate. It also shows your trading streak and a pre-market checklist to keep you disciplined every morning.

Which is the best free trade journal for Indian traders? +

TradeLog is the only trade journal for Indian traders that works automatically — no manual entry required. Other trade journals require you to manually enter each trade from Zerodha, Groww, or Upstox. TradeLog captures trades automatically the moment you place them.
